How it works

  1. Data cube: a 100 m grid with NDVI, NDBI, built-up share, canopy, water, roads, elevation, population and vulnerability.
  2. Heat model: XGBoost predicts land-surface temperature, tested against Linear Regression and Random Forest.
  3. Exposure index: heat above the city's cool reference × population × vulnerability weight.
  4. Feasible sites: cells with enough open land or road verge, excluding water, dense built-up and already wooded cells.
  5. Optimizer: picks a planting level for each site to maximise exposure reduction within the budget, the equity rule and the zone cap.
Data: simulated for this prototype. City layout, temperatures and population are synthetic; cost, survival and cooling values are assumptions.
